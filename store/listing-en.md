## Full description (max 4000)
Utterly lets you speak instead of type in almost any app.

A small microphone button floats over your screen. Tap a text field in a messaging app, social app, email, notes or your browser, tap the button, say what you want to write, and your words appear right where your cursor is. No switching keyboards.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open Utterly and turn on two things: microphone permission and the Utterly accessibility service.
2. Open any app and tap a text field.
3. Tap the mic button and speak.
4. Your words are inserted at the cursor.

FEATURES
• One mic button that works across apps
• Choose your speech language. Many languages are available; which ones work depends on the speech recognition engine on your phone
• Drag the button anywhere. Long-press it to hide it, and bring it back from the Utterly app
• Free, with no ads and no account

PRIVACY
Utterly has no internet access of its own and does not collect, store or share your data. It does not save what you say or what you type. Speech is converted to text by your phone's built-in speech recognition service (on most phones, Google's), which has its own privacy policy.

WHY UTTERLY NEEDS THE ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Utterly uses Android's Accessibility Service for two things only: to show the mic button on top of other apps, and to insert the text you dictated into the field you are using. It does not read, store or share what is on your screen. You choose to turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Some apps or fields may not accept inserted text.
• Speech recognition quality depends on your phone's speech engine and your language settings.
