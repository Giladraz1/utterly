# Demo video script (Accessibility declaration) — about 45 seconds

How to record on the Samsung: pull down the quick panel > Screen recorder.
Choose "No sound" (a microphone recording may interfere with speech recognition; I have not tested this).
Turn on "show taps/touches" if the recorder offers it. Use a notes app or an empty chat so no private content appears.
Start from a state where the Utterly accessibility service is OFF.

1. (0:00) Open Utterly. Show the status cards: accessibility not enabled, microphone permission.
2. (0:05) Tap "Grant permission" for the microphone and allow it.
3. (0:12) Tap "Open settings" on the Accessibility card. Go to Installed apps > Utterly Voice Typing. Pause on the system dialog that describes the permission, then turn the service on and confirm.
4. (0:25) Open a notes app or an empty chat. Tap the text field. The floating mic button is visible.
5. (0:30) Tap the mic button (it changes to its listening look), say a short sentence, and show the text appearing in the field.
6. (0:40) Return to Settings > Accessibility and turn the service off, to show the user stays in control.

Keep it under a minute. Upload it to YouTube as "Unlisted" and paste the link in the declaration form.
