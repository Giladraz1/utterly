Utterly Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Utterly ("the app") is an Android app that lets you dictate text by voice into the text fields of other apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: Utterly does not collect, store, or send your personal data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

What the app accesses and why

Microphone. When you tap the floating mic bubble, the app asks Android's built-in speech recognition service to listen and convert your speech to text. The microphone is used only while you have started a dictation. Utterly itself never receives or saves the audio; it receives only the resulting text.

Accessibility service. Utterly uses Android's Accessibility Service so it can (1) show the floating mic bubble on top of other apps, and (2) find the text field you are typing in and insert the dictated text there. The Accessibility permission technically allows an app to see content on screen. Utterly uses it only to locate the focused text field and insert text. It does not read, record, store, or transmit what is on your screen or what you type. Utterly only works when you turn the service on in Android's Accessibility settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

Clipboard. To insert text reliably, the app briefly places the dictated text on your clipboard, pastes it into the field, and then restores your previous clipboard content after about three seconds. While the text is on the clipboard, other apps or your keyboard's clipboard history may be able to see it, depending on your device.

Speech recognition is performed by Android, not by Utterly

The conversion of speech to text is done by the speech recognition service installed on your device, which on most phones is provided by Google. Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio on the device or on its provider's servers. That processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy (for Google, see policies.google.com/privacy), not by this policy. Utterly has no control over it. The app does not request internet access and cannot send data over the internet itself.

Data stored on your device

Utterly stores two settings locally on your phone: whether the mic bubble is shown, and the speech language you chose. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create accounts or ask for personal information.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share your data, because it does not collect any.
  • It does not log the text you dictate.

Children

Utterly is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
